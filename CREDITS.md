# Credits

## Artwork

The two companions (the Hermit; the Fool and his dog) and the four section
icons are cut from scans of the
original Rider–Waite–Smith tarot illustrated by **Pamela Colman Smith**,
first published by William Rider & Son in 1909. The scans are of the
"Pam-A" printing (c. 1910) and are hosted on Wikimedia Commons, where they are
marked as public domain:

| Used as | Card | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Hermit companion (5 puppet parts) | IX The Hermit | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:RWS_Tarot_09_Hermit.jpg |
| Fool companion and dog (7 puppet parts) | 0 The Fool | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:RWS_Tarot_00_Fool.jpg |
| Season icon (sunflowers) | XIX The Sun | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:RWS_Tarot_19_Sun.jpg |
| Next Sabbat icon (sun face) | XIX The Sun | same as above |
| Moon icon, app icon | XVIII The Moon | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:RWS_Tarot_18_Moon.jpg |
| Wheel icon | X Wheel of Fortune | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:RWS_Tarot_10_Wheel_of_Fortune.jpg |

The work was published before 1929 (public domain in the United States), and
Smith died in 1951, so the art is also out of copyright in countries using a
life + 70 years term. Later recoloured editions of the deck carry their own
copyright and **were not used**.

The cut-outs and crops are reproducible with `tools/prepare_puppet.py` and
`tools/prepare_icons.py` (Pillow only) from the scans above.
